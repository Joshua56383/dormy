University Dormitory Rulebook

A guide to maintaining a safe, respectful, and supportive residential community.

Official Code of Conduct

The university dormitory exists to provide students with a safe, clean, respectful, and supportive living environment that promotes academic success, discipline, and community responsibility.

  • Only currently enrolled students of the university are allowed to reside in the dormitory.
  • Residents must submit all required documents and follow the dorm application and renewal process.
  • Room assignments are non-transferable unless approved by dorm management.

  • Residents must show respect to fellow residents, dorm staff, and university property.
  • Any form of harassment, bullying, threats, or violence is strictly prohibited.
  • Disorderly conduct, vandalism, or intentional damage to property is not allowed.
  • Residents must follow university rules in addition to dormitory rules.

Quiet hours are enforced to support rest and study:

  • Weekdays: 10:00 PM – 6:00 AM
  • Weekends: 11:00 PM – 7:00 AM

Loud music, shouting, or disruptive activities during quiet hours are prohibited. Personal earphones are strongly recommended.

  • All visitors must log in and register with official ID at the lobby entrance.
  • Overnight stays by non-residents require advance formal written management approval.
  • Residents are personally responsible for the conduct and actions of their registered guests.

  • All residents must take part in their designated weekly cleaning rotations.
  • Common areas (hallways, shared kitchens, restrooms) must be kept clear of personal items.
  • Garbage must be properly sorted and disposed of in designated waste collection points.

  • Familiarize yourself with building evacuation routes posted on each floor.
  • Tampering with fire extinguishers, alarms, or smoke detectors is a severe disciplinary offense.
  • Report any maintenance, water leak, or electrical hazards to dorm management immediately.
